About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Marine Sciences at the University of Georgia Skidaway Institute of Oceanography, working under Dr. Sara Rivero-Calle. My research focuses on bio-optical oceanography, satellite ocean-color remote sensing, physical-biological interactions, and coastal water quality.

I integrate multi-mission satellite imagery, including NASA PACE, MODIS, VIIRS, Sentinel-3, and SeaHawk CubeSat, with high-performance computing, machine learning, and in situ radiometric and biogeochemical observations.
