## 🌊 About Me & Research Summary

I am a **Ph.D. Candidate in Marine Sciences** at the **University of Georgia (UGA) Skidaway Institute of Oceanography**, working under the advisement of Dr. Sara Rivero-Calle. My academic research centers on bio-optical oceanography, ocean color remote sensing, physical-biological interactions, and coastal water quality dynamics. 

I integrate multi-mission satellite observations (NASA PACE, SeaHawk CubeSat, MODIS, VIIRS, Sentinel-3) with high-performance computing (HPC), machine learning algorithms, and *in situ* radiometric and oceanographic field observations to address carbon cycling and coastal sediment dynamics across the South Atlantic Bight (SAB) and the Bay of Bengal[cite: 1].
