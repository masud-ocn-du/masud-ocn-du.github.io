Primary Research Interests

  • Phytoplankton Ecology & Biogeography: Community variability, primary productivity, and harmful algal blooms.
  • Bio-Optical Algorithms & Satellite Remote Sensing: Developing regional and global algorithms using NASA PACE, MODIS, VIIRS, Sentinel-3, and SeaHawk CubeSat data.
  • Marine Carbon Cycling & Sediment Transport: Quantifying Total Suspended Solids (TSS) and carbon sequestration in the South Atlantic Bight (SAB) and Bay of Bengal.
  • Physical-Biological Coupling: Observing interactions across river plumes, ocean fronts, mesoscale eddies, and tropical cyclones.

Active Projects & Algorithm Innovations

1. PACE OCI & SeaHawk CubeSat Radiometry

Evaluating hyperspectral radiometry performance of the low-cost SeaHawk CubeSat alongside NASA/NOAA satellite ocean color sensors and developing global TSS retrieval frameworks.

2. Sentinel-3 Machine Learning Algorithms for Case-2 Waters

Building machine learning algorithms for high-turbidity coastal waters in the northern Bay of Bengal to eliminate observational gaps in ocean modeling.
